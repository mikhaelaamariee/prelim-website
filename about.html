Meet the Research Team

An undergraduate policy research initiative from a BSIT program specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, examining the intersection of algorithmic fairness and Philippine higher education.

Mikhaela Marie L. Viray

Author · BSIT-3AIML
Writing 1 (Business Writing) — Prelim Project

This proposal was researched and written as a course requirement, applying evidence-based policy analysis to examine systemic inequities in AI academic integrity detection systems affecting Filipino students in higher education.

Combined Expertise

  • Machine Learning Systems and Algorithmic BiasTechnical grounding in how detection systems work, including perplexity-based classification and its known failure modes.
  • Higher Education Policy and CHED GovernanceUnderstanding of Philippine HEI policy frameworks and accreditation processes.
  • Technology Ethics and Procurement PolicyApplied research into AI governance frameworks relevant to institutional procurement and vendor accountability.

Tools & Resources

Academic research and literature review, APA 7th edition citation management, and website design and development for this proposal.

Feasibility Note

This proposal is designed for practical institutional adoption. Implementation requires no external funding, no specialized infrastructure, and is fully compatible with existing CHED accreditation and quality-assurance frameworks. It is a governance reform, not a funding initiative.
