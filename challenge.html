⚠ THE CHALLENGE

Understanding the Detection Gap

Three structural failures, technical bias, inconsistent enforcement, and ignored incentives, combine to create an inequitable system that harms non-native English writers while eroding the very integrity it claims to protect.

CAUSES
📊

Technical Detector Bias

AI detection systems are trained predominantly on native-English corpora. Non-native English writers' naturally lower "perplexity" and "burstiness" in sentence structure are algorithmically misread as signs of AI generation, without vendors publishing ESL-specific accuracy benchmarks.

61.3% average false-positive rate for non-native English writers (Liang et al., 2023, Patterns)
⚖️

Institutional Inconsistency and Double Standards

While students face investigation for suspected AI use, a majority of faculty already use AI themselves in their own teaching, without a unified policy governing both groups.

61% of faculty report having used AI in their own teaching (Digital Education Council, 2025)
⚡

Root Incentive Structures Ignored

Detection technology addresses the symptom, not the cause. Evasion tools continue to outpace classification methods, meaning enforcement alone cannot resolve dishonesty rooted in credential-driven incentives.

Evasion tools are evolving faster than classification methods can keep up (academic literature review, 2026)
EFFECTS
01

Wrongful Accusations of Academic Dishonesty

Even newer detectors retain a real false-positive gap for non-native writers. At scale, even a modest gap can translate into thousands of wrongful flags across a large student population (Copyleaks, 2024).

02

Erosion of Academic Trust

When students observe faculty using AI without the same disclosure or scrutiny expected of them, it damages trust in enforcement as a whole, a pattern already visible in ongoing PH cases (GMA News, 2023; Manila Times, 2026).

03

The Arms-Race Dynamic

As detection tools evolve, so do circumvention techniques. This adversarial cycle consumes institutional resources without resolving the underlying drivers of AI-assisted dishonesty.

04

Entrenched Systemic Inequity

Non-native English writers, already navigating English-centric academic conventions, face compounded barriers when legitimate writing patterns trigger disciplinary scrutiny.
