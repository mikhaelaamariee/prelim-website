📅 IMPLEMENTATION PLAN

A Phased Roadmap to Equity

An achievable, four-phase plan spanning 13+ months, designed to minimize institutional disruption while building durable equity infrastructure across all Philippine higher education institutions.

Phase 1 Months 1–3
Phase 2 Months 4–6
Phase 3 Months 7–12
Phase 4 Month 13+
1

Phase 1: Institutional Audit

Months 1–3

All Philippine HEIs conduct a comprehensive internal audit of currently deployed AI detection tools, documenting vendor contracts, policy coverage, and all recorded AI-flagged academic misconduct incidents over the preceding three academic years.

  • ✓Catalogue all active AI detection tool subscriptions and contract terms
  • ✓Document existing academic integrity policy coverage for AI tools
  • ✓Compile anonymized data on AI-flagged cases and outcomes (2022–2024)
  • ✓Survey faculty on current AI detection workflows and confidence levels
  • ✓Identify gaps in ESL-protective language across institutional policies
  • ✓Submit audit report to CHED by end of Month 3
📄 Deliverable: Institutional Audit Report submitted to CHED
2

Phase 2: Policy Standardization

Months 4–6

The Commission on Higher Education convenes a multi-stakeholder working group, including faculty representatives, student advocates, ESL specialists, and legal counsel, to draft a unified Model Academic Integrity and AI-Use Policy template for national adoption.

  • ✓CHED establishes working group with representation from at least 12 HEIs
  • ✓Draft model policy with symmetric faculty-student AI use provisions
  • ✓Incorporate ESL false-positive protections and mandatory human review clauses
  • ✓Publish model policy for 30-day public comment period
  • ✓Finalize and release model policy with implementation guidance for HEIs
  • ✓Provide faculty training module on ESL writing recognition
📄 Deliverable: CHED Model AI Integrity Policy Template (publicly available)
3

Phase 3: Vendor Accountability

Months 7–12

HEIs require all AI detection tool vendors to submit comprehensive ESL false-positive rate documentation as a binding condition of contract renewal. Vendors who fail to comply are removed from approved procurement lists and referred to CHED for review.

  • ✓Issue formal vendor disclosure requirements to all active AI tool providers
  • ✓Establish standardized ESL false-positive benchmark testing protocol
  • ✓Require vendors to submit documentation by end of Month 9
  • ✓Review submissions against standardized criteria with external experts
  • ✓Publish Vendor Compliance Registry on CHED website
  • ✓Terminate procurement relationships with non-compliant vendors
📄 Deliverable: Public Vendor Compliance Registry with ESL accuracy ratings
4

Phase 4: Ongoing Monitoring and Reporting

Month 13 onward

CHED establishes a permanent Annual Academic Integrity Equity Report tracking false-positive incident rates, policy adherence, and vendor compliance across all Philippine HEIs, publicly released each academic year to maintain accountability and enable evidence-based policy revision.

  • ✓Annual data collection from all HEIs on AI-flagged cases and outcomes
  • ✓Disaggregated analysis by student demographic and language background
  • ✓Public release of Annual Academic Integrity Equity Report
  • ✓Bi-annual review of vendor compliance and tool accuracy benchmarks
  • ✓Mechanism for HEIs to report emerging AI detection concerns to CHED
  • ✓Continuous policy revision cycle based on evidence and stakeholder feedback
📄 Deliverable: Annual Academic Integrity Equity Report (public, recurring)
