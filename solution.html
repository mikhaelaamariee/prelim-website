The Transparency & Equity-Audit Framework

A two-pillar institutional reform framework requiring vendor accountability for ESL bias disclosure and symmetric AI-use governance that applies the same rules to students and faculty alike. Designed for adoption by CHED and individual Philippine HEIs.

I

Vendor Transparency and ESL Disclosure

Procurement-conditional accountability requirements
  • Published false-positive rates for ESL and multilingual writing samples, annually updated
  • Training data provenance reports disclosing language diversity and geographic representation
  • Model update changelogs with accuracy benchmark methodology per language group
  • Independent third-party audit certification every two years as a condition of HEI procurement
  • Mandatory grace period and human review for all AI-flagged cases involving international or ESL-designated students
II

Unified AI-Use Policy: Students and Faculty

Symmetric, evidence-based institutional governance
  • A single, symmetrical AI-use policy that applies identical standards and scrutiny to both student work and faculty pedagogy
  • Mandatory human expert review before any AI-generated alert proceeds to formal disciplinary proceedings
  • Structured appeal pathways staffed by faculty trained in ESL writing assessment
  • Published institutional statistics on AI detection alerts, outcomes, and demographic breakdown, released annually
  • Faculty accountability clauses covering AI-assisted grading, feedback generation, and syllabus writing
10

SDG 10 Alignment: Reduced Inequalities

TARGET 10.2

Empower and promote social, economic, and political inclusion of all. This framework removes algorithmically embedded barriers that structurally exclude multilingual Filipino students from equal participation in academic assessment.

TARGET 10.3

Ensure equal opportunity and reduce inequalities of outcome. Vendor disclosure requirements and symmetrical policy enforcement directly address discriminatory outcomes embedded in current AI detection workflows.

Direct Benefits of Adoption

✓

Reduced Wrongful Accusations

Mandatory human review gates and ESL-calibrated thresholds significantly reduce erroneous disciplinary proceedings against legitimate writers.

✓

Restored Institutional Trust

Transparent, symmetric policies rebuild student and faculty confidence in the fairness of academic integrity systems.

✓

Smarter Vendor Procurement

Disclosure requirements give institutions the data they need to make evidence-based decisions about which AI tools are actually fit for purpose in ESL contexts.

✓

Regional Policy Leadership

Philippine HEIs that adopt this framework position themselves as regional leaders in equitable AI governance in higher education.
